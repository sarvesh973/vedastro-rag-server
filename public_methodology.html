The honest pitch

Most AI astrology apps do this: take your birth chart, feed it to a large language model, and let the model write something astrology-flavoured. That's literary fiction in Sanskrit terminology.

Moksha does it differently. Your chart is computed deterministically. Then it's evaluated against 200+ hand-encoded classical rules. Only matched rules are sent to the language model, which is forbidden from inventing predictions outside them. Every claim cites the verse it came from.

We can't promise accurate predictions — no astrology system can prove that. What we can promise is that every prediction traces to a specific verse from a real classical text, applied to your actual chart by an algorithm anyone can audit.

Architecture

Three layers, in order.

1.

Chart computation

Birth date, time, and place go through standard Vedic astronomy. We compute the natal chart (D1) plus 15 divisional charts (D2 through D60), the Vimshottari dasha tree, Ashtakavarga bindu scores, Shadbala planetary strengths, the Jaimini karaka chain (Atmakaraka through Darakaraka), 18 classical yogas, and current planetary transits with Sade Sati / double-transit detection. This is all math — same inputs, same outputs, every time. No language model involved.

2.

Rule evaluation

When you ask a question, an LLM classifies it into one of nine domains (marriage, career, wealth, health, children, education, foreign, family, spirituality). The rule engine then runs every rule for that domain against your computed chart. Each matched rule returns its source citation, intensity score, polarity, and evidence from your chart. The top matches are passed to the next layer.

3.

Language presentation

Gemini receives the matched rules with strict instructions: present them in conversational Hinglish or English; never invent a prediction outside this list; cite the book naturally; if only weak rules matched, say the chart doesn't show strong signals on this topic. The model's job is rewording, not reasoning. The reasoning happened in step 2.

Indexed classical sources

Three foundational texts, embedded into a searchable vector database (~935 verse-level chunks).

Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra
363 chunks

The foundational Vedic astrology text attributed to sage Parashara. Covers planetary characters, house effects, all major dasha systems, yogas, divisional charts, and predictive techniques.

Phaladeepika
186 chunks

Mantreswara's classical work on practical prediction, distilled from the Parashari tradition. Particularly strong on transits, dashas, and house-by-house effects.

Saravali
386 chunks

Kalyana Varma's 10th-century compendium of yogas and predictive combinations. Especially strong on Raja Yogas, Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas, and Ashtakavarga.

How we validate

A 45-event test suite drawn from documented celebrity charts. Re-run on every rule change.

For each event in the suite — Tendulkar's 1995 marriage, Modi's 2014 PM oath, Aishwarya Rai's 2007 wedding, Steve Jobs's 2003 cancer diagnosis, and 41 others — we compute the celebrity's chart, identify what planetary period was active at the actual event date, and check whether our rule engine's timing predictions would have fired in that window. We report three metrics:

STRICT27%

2 or more strong timing rules fire AND their polarity aligns with the event. The defensible accuracy number. Random-chance baseline for a 9-dasha system is ~11%, so the engine performs 2.5× chance.

BASIC89%

At least one timing rule fires for the event's planetary period. The "your chart had supporting signals" softer number — still meaningful, but a looser bar.

COMBINED100%

Any matched rule (timing or lifetime-structural). Trivially high — natal charts always have structural indicators. Reported for completeness.

What we don't claim

  • ✗That Vedic astrology is scientifically proven. It isn't. We're applying classical technique transparently — not making medical, legal, or financial claims.
  • ✗That we predict death dates, severe illnesses, or specific accident timings. We deliberately don't, regardless of what classical texts say. The reading is for guidance and self-reflection.
  • ✗That an app replaces a qualified human astrologer for major life decisions. We're a daily companion and educational tool.
  • ✗That our accuracy is 89%+. The honest number is 27% strict — meaningfully better than chance, but far from oracular.
